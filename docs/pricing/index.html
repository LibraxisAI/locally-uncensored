What LU Cloud costs

The desktop app is free and always will be. Everything below buys hosted models: the ones we run, so you do not need a graphics card. Every plan carries the whole catalogue. There is no model behind a higher tier.

Payment happens on lu-labs.ai. This page states the numbers; the checkout there is the only thing that takes money.

What you get on every plan

  • 24 of the 46 cloud chat models we measured answer in full without refusing, and only those carry the No refusals mark. Measured on 2026-09-10 by asking each model directly, not guessed from its name. The mark sits in the model picker. The catalogue holds 47 chat models; the one that joined after the run carries no mark until it is measured. We add no content filter of our own to text, on any plan.
  • Flash chat runs unmetered inside the apps, up to 500,000 combined input and output tokens per account per day. 12 models are in that class. It resets at 00:00 UTC.
  • Adult image and video once you turn your own filter off in the account settings, after confirming you are 18 or older. Ten video endpoints and three image models run without a built-in content restriction.
  • The same 10 image models and the same video catalogue on every plan, plus music, speech and character training.

What is not included

  • Unmetered Flash needs an active paid plan. Accounts without an active plan keep paying credits for Flash exactly like for any other model. There is no free tier and no trial allowance.
  • API keys always pay credits, including on Flash models. The unmetered path is the app, not the endpoint.
  • Cloud is not local privacy. Hosted requests leave your machine and run at external inference providers. If that matters for a piece of work, run it locally: the desktop app does that for free.
  • Two lines no setting moves. Material involving minors is refused on every request. You may not upload a photograph of a real, identifiable person without their consent.

Prefer your own hardware?

The desktop app runs local models on Windows and Linux at no cost, and it is the same app: local and cloud sit side by side in one picker. There is no Mac build; on a Mac the hosted studio runs in the browser instead.
